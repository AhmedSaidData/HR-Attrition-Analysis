 HR Attrition Analysis

Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition, retention, and headcount trends across departments, business units, and zones.

## 📌 Problem

Understanding why employees leave, where attrition is highest, and how headcount changes over time, to support retention decisions.

## 📊 Dashboard Metrics

- **Attrition rate** by department and business unit
- **Retention rate** and average tenure
- **Headcount growth** trend over time
- **Active headcount %** by division
- **Employee distribution** by zone
- **Active vs terminated** breakdown by employment type

## 🔑 Key Measures

- 25+ DAX measures covering attrition, retention, headcount change, percentage of total headcount, zone breakdowns, and tenure
- Time intelligence using `SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR` for year-over-year comparisons

## 🛠️ Tools

Power BI, DAX, Power Query
